My Story.

Since childhood, I had always been curious in observing the world around me. Growing up, I gradually starting noticing the small problems in everyday life. Upon realization, I really started enjoying exploring unfamiliar ideas and developing new perspectives so I can come up with well rounded solutions. This mindset further bloomed in the summer of 2025, when my family and I went on vacation in Cambodia. Before going on that vacation, my focus was on small issues that may cause minor inconveniences in everyday life. In Cambodia, I got the chance to talk to locals and they told me the children there only go to school once every two days, and their school day is merely half day, as for the other half of the day the children have to go home to help with their parents' businesses. Additionally, they told me that not many children have the chance to even attend high school, let alone post secondary education, becuase they are pressured to work and make money early to take a toll off the family. On top of that, I got the chance to tour a neighbourhood with bamboo houses that were built on water. This experience made me realize that certain things I considered normal in my own life may be a privilege that others may not have. It was this experience that pushed me to start taking intiative towards addressing the bigger issues in the world. This drove my interest Academically, I want to learn more about financial structures and how they influence the world around me, especially how they impact people's access to opportunities. Eventually, I want to use that knowledge and any financial resources I may have to support educational initiatives around the world, particularly for children from less advantaged backgrounds.
