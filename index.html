My Story.

Since childhood, I have always been curious about observing the world around me. Growing up, I gradually noticed the small problems in everyday life and started enjoying exploring unfamiliar ideas and developing new perspectives. My mindset got enriched in the summer of 2025, when my family and I went on vacation in Cambodia. Before going on that vacation, my focus was on fixing small issues that cause minor inconveniences. In Cambodia, I got the chance to talk to locals, and they told me the children only get to go to school for half the day because they have to go home to help with housework during the other half of the day. Even so, a very small number of children get the chance to attend high school, let alone postsecondary education. On top of that, I got the chance to tour a neighbourhood with bamboo houses that were built on water. These experiences made me realize that certain things I considered normal in my own life may be a privilege that others may not have. Coming back to Canada, I started taking more initiative towards addressing the bigger issues in the world. It also drove my interest in learning more about financial structures and how they influence the world around me, especially how they impact people's access to opportunities. Eventually, I want to use that knowledge and any financial resources I may have to support educational initiatives around the world, particularly for children from less advantaged backgrounds.
